      Costs and weather to watch

        Seasonal pattern

        No consistent seasonal pattern. We compared each month's R4L price with the 12-month average around it, for England and Wales steers and heifers, from 2014 to 2026.

        From 2014 to 2019, prices tended to be lower in spring and higher in autumn. Since 2020 it has mostly been the other way round. Over the whole period, no month averages more than about 1% above or below the 12-month average, so time of year isn't a reliable guide to where prices will go.

        Source: AHDB, GB deadweight cattle prices (monthly). Worked out by this site in October 2026.

            Where the information comes from

            Each point links to its own source: AHDB (the Agriculture and Horticulture Development Board, the farming levy body), Farmers Weekly, Agriland, the Irish Farmers' Association, Rabobank (a farming bank, as reported by Agriland), the Met Office, the NFU (National Farmers' Union, as reported by FarmingUK), and AHDB's price data used elsewhere on this site.

            Steers and heifers only. Cows and young bulls are not included.

            All prices are market averages. What any one animal makes depends on its weight, grade, buyer and the deductions taken off, so your own prices will differ. This site is for information only. It is not advice on when to buy or sell.
